# Project notes

## Brand: כח התערבות
- The name is always spelled **כח התערבות** — כח without ו, and התערבות with a single ה (never "כוח", never "ההתערבות"). Applies everywhere: captions (even when the speaker says "כוח ההתערבות"), titles, end cards, copy.
- Tagline: "לובי ציוני לאומי". Lobby of the "על המשמעות" podcast.
- Logo source: Google Drive `לוגו.jpg.jpeg` (navy #21366D + gold #EFC73E). On dark backgrounds use the reversed version in `deliverables/brand/koach-hitarvut_logo_on-dark.png` (built by `deliverables/source/logo_rev.py`).
